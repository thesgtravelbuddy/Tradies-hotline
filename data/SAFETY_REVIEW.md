# Safety wording to review before launch

Every message below is what the AI will tell a customer if a danger sign is mentioned. A licensed tradesperson or the relevant regulator should approve each one. Many sources are Victorian; state numbers/rules need checking.

## Air Conditioning

- **Split system not cooling** (basis: sources): If you smell burning or see smoke, switch the aircon off at the wall or isolator only if safe, keep clear of the unit and call 000 if there is fire.
- **Air conditioner leaking water** (basis: mixed): If water is near power points or lights, or the ceiling is bulging, switch the aircon off at the isolator and the circuit breaker only if safe, and keep away from the wet area.
- **Air conditioner noisy** (basis: mixed): If there is a burning smell, smoke or sparks, switch off at the wall or isolator only if safe, keep clear and call 000 if there is fire.
- **Air conditioner smells** (basis: mixed): If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall or isolator only if safe, leave the area and call 000 if there is fire. If you smell gas, go outside and call the gas emergency number.
- **Air conditioner won't turn on** (basis: sources): If the breaker keeps tripping, or you smell burning, leave it switched off and do not keep resetting it. Call 000 if there is fire or smoke.
- **Air conditioner error code or flashing light** (basis: sources): If there is a burning smell or smoke with the fault, switch off at the wall or isolator only if safe and keep clear. Call 000 if there is fire.
- **Ducted air conditioning fault** (basis: mixed): If you smell burning or see smoke, switch the system off at the wall controller or isolator only if safe and call 000 if there is fire. Keep away from wet ceilings near lights.
- **Evaporative cooler problem** (basis: mixed): If you smell burning, or water is near electrics, switch the cooler off at the wall or isolator only if safe. Do not climb on the roof.
- **Ice or frost on air conditioner** (basis: mixed): Switch the aircon off at the remote to let the ice melt. If water is near electrics, switch off at the isolator only if safe.
- **Aircon tripping the circuit breaker** (basis: mixed): Do not keep resetting the breaker. Leave the aircon off, and if there is a burning smell, sparks or smoke, keep clear and call 000.
- **Outdoor unit not running but indoor unit is on** (basis: mixed): Do not touch the fan. If there is a burning smell or smoke, switch off at the isolator only if safe and call 000 if there is fire.

## Carpentry

- **Sliding door stuck or off track** (basis: knowledge): If the door is off its track or glass is cracked, keep people and pets clear and do not slide it.
- **Window stuck or will not open** (basis: knowledge): If a bedroom window that may be needed for escape is stuck shut, tell the tradie it is a safety priority.
- **Broken or cracked window glass** (basis: knowledge): Keep people and pets away from broken glass. Do not touch edges. If it was a break-in, contact police first.
- **Rotten timber (frames, fascia, windowsills)** (basis: knowledge): If timber supporting a roof, balcony or stairs is soft or sagging, keep people away from it.
- **Termite damaged timber** (basis: mixed): Do not disturb active termite workings. Keep people away from sagging floors or damaged structure.
- **Decking repair or rot** (basis: mixed): If a deck feels unstable or a rail is loose, keep people off the deck, especially children and the elderly.
- **Stairs or handrail loose or damaged** (basis: mixed): Avoid using the stairs or leaning on the rail if it moves. Keep children and the elderly away until checked.
- **Wardrobe or sliding robe doors** (basis: knowledge): Do not lean on a loose mirrored door. Keep people and pets away if it could fall.
- **Squeaky or lifting floorboards** (basis: knowledge): If the floor feels like it will give way, keep people off that area.
- **Laminate or timber floor repair** (basis: knowledge): If water is still leaking, turn off water at the mains if safe to do so and call a plumber first.
- **Roof or pergola timber sagging** (basis: knowledge): Keep people away from under the structure, especially in wind or rain.
- **Door frame or jamb damage after break-in** (basis: knowledge): If you suspect a break-in, contact police first. Keep clear of damaged frames.
- **Water-damaged timber after a leak or flood** (basis: knowledge): If water is still coming in, turn off the water at the mains if safe, and call a plumber.

- **Uneven or sunken pavers** (basis: mixed): Mark or barricade raised edges to avoid trips.
- **Subsidence or structural cracks** (basis: knowledge): If cracks are widening quickly or a wall looks to be leaning, leave the area and tell us now.
- **Concrete surface flaking or rust staining** (basis: knowledge): Keep clear of overhead concrete that may fall.

## Damp & Mould

- **Water stain on ceiling** (basis: mixed): Please don't climb on the roof. Keep clear of the stained area; if it sags or drips near lights, avoid using those lights and switch them off if you can do so safely.
- **Sagging or bulging wet ceiling** (basis: mixed): Keep everyone out of the room and away from the ceiling. Turn off lights below at the wall switch only if you can do it from a dry spot. Don't poke it. Call 000 if it falls.
- **Damp or mouldy walls** (basis: mixed): Don't scrub dry or disturb large mouldy areas; spores spread. People with asthma, allergies or weak immunity, and young or older people, should stay out. Please don't climb to check roofs or gutters.
- **Rising damp** (basis: sources): Please don't scrape or sand damp walls or salt deposits in old homes; dust may contain old paint or other hazards. Keep stored items off wet floors and avoid damp skirting near power points.
- **Condensation and window mould** (basis: sources): Don't dry-brush mould, as that spreads spores. Anyone with asthma or weak immunity should stay clear. Don't use unflued gas heaters in sealed rooms.
- **Bathroom ceiling mould** (basis: mixed): Don't dry-scrub mould. Avoid standing under a bubbling ceiling, and keep wet hands away from fan or light switches. People with asthma should stay away while it's present.
- **Musty smell but no visible mould** (basis: knowledge): Don't cut into walls or ceilings to look. People with breathing problems should stay in another room. Please don't climb into the roof space to search.
- **Rain coming through external wall** (basis: mixed): Keep power points and appliances away from wet walls, and don't use them if water is running down. Please don't climb to inspect the roof or gutters.

## Drainage

- **Blocked bathroom, shower or laundry drain** (basis: mixed): Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Blocked sewer drain or tree roots** (basis: mixed): Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Sewage overflow** (basis: sources): Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends. If overflow is large or ongoing, also call your water authority's 24-hour line.
- **Septic tank or onsite system problems** (basis: sources): Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends. Keep stock and children away from wet patches.
- **Overflow relief gully bubbling or overflowing** (basis: sources): Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Blocked stormwater drain or pit** (basis: mixed): Keep away from pooled water near power outlets or switchboards, and turn off power at the board only if it is safe to reach.
- **Overflow relief gully missing, buried or damaged** (basis: sources): Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Stormwater getting into the sewer, overflow in wet weather** (basis: sources): Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Sewer smell inside or outside the house** (basis: mixed): If the smell is very strong or makes anyone dizzy or sick, ventilate and leave the area, then call emergency services if symptoms continue.
- **Sump or drainage pump not working** (basis: knowledge): If water is near power points or the switchboard, stay out of it and do not touch the pump; turn power off only if the switchboard is dry and safe to reach.

## Electrical

- **Safety switch keeps tripping** (basis: sources): If there is burning smell, sparks or water near electrics, keep clear and call 000 if fire. Do not touch wiring or wet fittings. Leave tripped circuits off until an electrician has checked them.
- **Power out to part of the house** (basis: sources): If you smell burning or see smoke or sparks, keep clear and call 000 if fire. Do not open wall plates or touch wiring. Report the issue to a licensed electrician.
- **Whole house power outage** (basis: sources): Stay well clear of fallen powerlines and anything touching them; call 000. If there is a burning smell or sparks, keep away. Only a licensed electrician should check wiring. Report street outages to your distributor.
- **Power point not working** (basis: sources): Do not use a power point that is hot, scorched, cracked or wet. Switch it off at the board if safe to reach. Do not open it or touch wiring.
- **Sparking or burnt power point** (basis: sources): Keep clear and do not touch. If safe to reach, switch off at the switchboard. If there is fire or smoke, leave and call 000. Never touch wiring or use the power point.
- **Burning smell from electrics** (basis: sources): If you see smoke or flames, leave and call 000. Otherwise, switch off at the switchboard only if safe to reach, keep clear, and call an electrician. Never touch wiring or hot fittings.
- **Lights flickering or dimming** (basis: mixed): If you notice burning smell, sparking or crackling, switch off at the switchboard only if safe to reach and keep clear. Do not touch wiring. Call 000 if fire.
- **Light not working** (basis: knowledge): If the fitting is hot, scorched, wet or smells of burning, leave it switched off and keep clear. Do not touch wiring. Call 000 if there is fire.
- **Downlights tripping or failing** (basis: sources): If you smell burning or see smoke from the ceiling, switch off at the switchboard only if safe to reach, leave the area and call 000 if fire. Do not touch wiring or fittings.
- **Ceiling fan install or fault** (basis: knowledge): If the fan is loose, smells burnt or sparks, switch it off at the wall and the switchboard only if safe, keep people clear and call 000 if fire. Do not touch wiring or wobbling parts.
- **Switchboard upgrade or old fuse box** (basis: mixed): Do not open the switchboard or touch components. If there is burning smell, sparks or heat, stay clear, switch off the main switch only if safe to reach, and call 000 if fire.
- **No safety switch fitted** (basis: sources): Safety switches protect against shock but may not cover every circuit. Do not open the board. If you get any shock or tingle, stop using that item and call a licensed electrician.
- **Smoke alarm beeping or not working** (basis: mixed): If you see or smell smoke or fire, leave and call 000. Do not remove or disable alarms. If one is sounding unexpectedly, keep everyone safe and arrange an electrician.
- **Power cut: house or street problem** (basis: sources): Stay well clear of fallen powerlines and anything touching them; call 000. Report outages to your distributor using the number on your bill. Only licensed electricians should check wiring.
- **Storm or flood damage to electrics** (basis: sources): Stay clear of fallen powerlines and floodwater near electrics; call 000. Do not switch on wet circuits or appliances. A licensed electrician must check wiring after storm or flood damage.
- **Buzzing, humming or crackling electrics** (basis: mixed): If buzzing comes with burning smell, heat, sparks or smoke, leave it switched off if safe, keep clear and call 000 if fire. Do not open fittings or touch wiring.
- **Shock or tingle from tap, appliance or fitting** (basis: sources): Do not touch or re-test the item; keep everyone away. Call 000 if anyone is hurt. Report the shock or tingle to your electricity distributor and arrange a licensed electrician. A tingle is a warning.
- **Hot switch, plug or cord** (basis: sources): Unplug or switch off only if safe and dry, stay clear, and keep cords unwound. Do not stack plugs. If smoke or burning, call 000. Do not touch wiring.
- **Damaged wires, meter box or service line** (basis: sources): Stay well clear of fallen or low powerlines and anything they touch; call 000 and your distributor. Do not touch, prune trees or approach. Only authorised workers should fix supply lines.
- **Generator connection or backup power** (basis: sources): Never connect a generator to your home wiring yourself. Only a licensed electrician may do it. Run generators outdoors, away from windows, and never plug them into a power point.
- **Install extra power points** (basis: knowledge): If you see burn marks, smell burning or the socket feels hot, switch off at the board and keep clear until an electrician checks it.
- **Oven or cooktop hardwiring** (basis: mixed): If the old oven or cooktop smells of burning, sparks or trips the power, switch it off at the board and do not use it until checked.
- **Outdoor lighting or power** (basis: knowledge): Keep away from wet or damaged outdoor fittings and cables. Switch off at the switchboard if safe. Do not touch exposed wires.
- **Solar system not producing** (basis: sources): Only read the inverter screen or app. Do not open the inverter, touch roof wiring or climb up. If burning smell, scorching or arcing, keep clear and call 000.
- **Generator install or fault** (basis: mixed): Never run a generator indoors, in a garage or enclosed space. Never plug it into a wall socket or the switchboard. If anyone feels dizzy or unwell, get into fresh air and call 000.
- **Storm or lightning damage** (basis: sources): Stay 8 to 10 metres from fallen powerlines and call 000 and your network. Do not touch damaged cables, wet switchboards or solar equipment. Do not turn power on if damage is visible.
- **Electric shock or tingle** (basis: sources): If anyone is hurt or unwell, call 000. Do not touch the appliance, tap or switchboard. Unplug only if dry and safe, switch off at the board, and call a licensed electrician.
- **Hot water element or electric hot water fault** (basis: knowledge): If water is leaking near electrics or you smell burning, switch off the hot water circuit at the board and keep clear. Do not open covers.
- **Powerboard overload / blown fuse** (basis: knowledge): If you smell burning or see scorch marks, switch off at the board, unplug items if safe and keep clear. Do not keep resetting a breaker that trips again.
- **Solar panels damaged by hail or storm** (basis: sources): Do not climb on the roof or touch damaged panels or cables. If it is safe, follow the shutdown label at the inverter. Call a licensed electrician and photograph from the ground.
- **Dim or flickering lights across the house** (basis: sources): Do not touch the switchboard or metal in the home. Turn off easy-to-reach power points, unplug appliances and call your electricity distributor immediately.
- **Safety switch won't reset** (basis: sources): If the safety switch will not reset, do not keep trying. Call a licensed electrician. Do not touch the board if wet or burnt-smelling.
- **Damaged solar battery** (basis: sources): Do not switch on a damaged battery and treat it as live. Keep clear and call a licensed electrician. If smoke or fire, leave and call 000.

## Fencing

- **Fence leaning or falling over** (basis: mixed): Keep people and pets away from the fence. Do not lean on it or push it.
- **Storm-damaged fence** (basis: mixed): Stay clear of fallen trees and fences near power lines. Keep children and pets away from broken areas.
- **Gate sagging or not closing** (basis: mixed): If this is a pool gate that does not close and latch, keep children away from the pool until fixed.
- **Retaining wall problem** (basis: mixed): Keep people, children and pets away from below and above the wall. Do not dig or add weight near it.
- **Pool or balcony safety barrier repair** (basis: mixed): Keep children away from the pool or balcony until the barrier is fixed. Supervise closely or block access temporarily.

## Garage Doors

- **Garage or roller door won't open or close** (basis: sources): Stay clear of a stuck, sagging or partly fallen door. Do not touch springs or cables. Keep people and pets away.
- **Garage door spring or cable broken** (basis: sources): Do not touch the spring, cables or door. Keep people and pets clear and do not stand under it. Call a technician.
- **Garage door off track or crooked** (basis: mixed): Do not push or lift a jammed or crooked door. Keep clear and keep people and pets away.
- **Garage opener motor hums but door won't move** (basis: sources): Do not use the opener if it grinds or the door looks unbalanced. Keep clear of the door.

## Gas

- **Gas smell or suspected leak** (basis: sources): If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **Gas appliance not working** (basis: mixed): Gas smell, hissing, soot or headache near a gas appliance: turn it off, open windows, get everyone outside, avoid switches and flames, call your gas emergency number, or 000 if anyone is unwell.
- **Gas heater fault or fumes concern** (basis: sources): Headache, dizziness, nausea or confusion near a gas appliance may be carbon monoxide. Turn it off, open doors and windows, get everyone outside, and call 000 or Nurse-on-Call 1300 60 60 24.
- **LPG bottle or regulator problem** (basis: sources): If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **New gas point or gas appliance install** (basis: mixed): If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **Portable or BBQ gas bottle leaking or no gas flow** (basis: sources): If you smell gas or hear hissing, put out flames, keep sparks away, turn the bottle valve off only if safe, move people away and call the supplier emergency number or 000.

## General Triage

- **Something is wrong but I don't know the trade** (basis: knowledge): If there is a gas smell, fire, sparks, flooding or someone is hurt, call 000 or the relevant emergency number now.
- **Strange smell** (basis: mixed): If you smell gas, don't use switches or flames, open doors, leave, and call your gas emergency number or 000. Burning smell or smoke: switch off power if safe, leave, call 000.
- **Water stain on ceiling** (basis: knowledge): If the ceiling is sagging, dripping near lights or you hear flowing water, keep out of that room, switch off power at the switchboard if safe, and call a licensed tradie.
- **Noise in the walls** (basis: knowledge): If you hear buzzing or crackling from switches, or smell gas or burning, stop using that area, switch off power if safe, and call 000 for fire or gas.
- **Storm damage** (basis: mixed): Stay at least 8 metres from fallen wires and anything they touch. Storm help: SES 132 500. If life is in danger or there is fire, call 000. Do not touch a damaged switchboard.
- **Power or water cut in part of the house** (basis: mixed): Do not touch a damaged or wet switchboard. If you smell burning, see sparks or water near electrics, keep away and call 000. After storms, call a licensed electrician.
- **Damp or mould on walls** (basis: knowledge): If anyone has breathing problems around the mould, avoid the area and seek medical advice. Don't dry-scrub or disturb heavy mould.
- **Cracks in walls or floors** (basis: knowledge): If a crack is wide, growing quickly, or a wall is leaning or bulging, keep out of that area and call a builder or structural engineer.
- **Smell of gas** (basis: mixed): Don't use switches, phones indoors, or flames. Open doors, leave, and keep others away. Call your gas emergency number from outside, or 000.
- **Hidden leak or high water bill** (basis: knowledge): If water is flowing near electrics or flooding, turn off the main tap, and the power if safe. Keep away from wet switchboards and cords.
- **Water coming in after rain** (basis: mixed): Stay clear of water near power points and don't walk through floodwater. For flood help call SES 132 500; if life is threatened, call 000.

## Gutters

- **Gutters overflowing** (basis: mixed): Please don't go up a ladder or onto the roof in wet weather. Water pouring near the house can soak walls and slabs; keep clear of wet ground near power points.
- **Gutter leaking or rusted through** (basis: mixed): Please don't climb a ladder to patch it. Keep clear of drips onto steps or entries to avoid slips, and don't scrub or break old cement-type gutters or sheeting that may contain asbestos.
- **Gutter replacement** (basis: mixed): Please don't climb a ladder to hold up or fix hanging gutters. Keep people clear beneath any gutter that is sagging or pulling away. Old cement-type gutters shouldn't be broken or cut by anyone.
- **Downpipe blocked, broken or disconnected** (basis: mixed): Please don't climb to unclip or force a downpipe. Keep clear of water pooling at the house, and keep it away from power points and doorways.
- **Fascia or eaves rotting or damaged** (basis: mixed): Please don't climb up to push or pull at the boards. Keep people clear under sagging or falling timber. Don't sand, drill or scrape old eaves sheeting; it may contain asbestos.
- **Leaf guard or gutter guard installation** (basis: knowledge): Please don't climb a ladder to clean or fit guards yourself. Never lean ladders on gutters. Old cement-type gutters or roofing shouldn't be touched or broken.

## Pest Control

- **Rats or mice** (basis: mixed): If you smell burning or see sparks, switch off at the switchboard and call an electrician. Do not touch droppings with bare hands; keep children and pets away.
- **Ants** (basis: mixed): If anyone is stung and has trouble breathing, swelling of face or throat, or collapses, call 000.
- **Termites or white ants** (basis: mixed): Do not disturb the area or spray it yourself, as this can make termites move and harder to find. If floors or beams look unsafe, keep clear.
- **Spiders** (basis: mixed): Suspected funnel-web bite (large dark spider): call 000, keep the person still, apply a firm bandage. Child, elderly or severe pain, sweating or breathing trouble from any bite: call 000. Do not catch the spider.
- **Wasps or bees nest** (basis: mixed): If stung and unwell, with swelling of face or throat or trouble breathing, call 000. Keep away from nests and swarms, and keep children and pets clear. Do not spray or knock the nest.
- **Possums in the roof** (basis: mixed): Possums are protected native wildlife; do not trap, poison or harm them. Sick or injured one: call a wildlife rescue group. Burning smell or sparks: switch off at the switchboard and call an electrician.
- **Fleas, ticks or mites** (basis: mixed): Tick on a person: do not pull or squeeze it; seek medical advice. Trouble breathing, face swelling or collapse after a bite: call 000.
- **Mosquitoes or flies outdoors** (basis: knowledge): If anyone has a fever, rash or joint pain after mosquito bites, see a doctor.
- **Birds or bats roosting** (basis: knowledge): Bats and many birds are protected wildlife; do not harm them. Do not handle a bat with bare hands; call a vet or wildlife carer and see a doctor if scratched or bitten.
- **Snakes or lizards near the house** (basis: mixed): Snake bite, even if painless or no marks: call 000, keep the person completely still, firmly bandage the limb. Keep clear of the snake; do not try to catch or kill it. Keep pets and children away.
- **Fire ants suspected** (basis: mixed): Do not touch or poke the nest; fire ants swarm. If stung with trouble breathing, facial swelling or faintness, call 000. Report suspected fire ants on 13 22 68 (NSW: NSW Government).
- **Flying termite swarm indoors** (basis: mixed): Do not spray or disturb the area. Switch off lights, keep the swarm sealed in one room if possible, and keep a sample or photo.
- **Dead animal smell in wall or roof** (basis: knowledge): If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas emergency line. Do not enter sealed roof spaces without protective gear.
- **Bee swarm or hive in a wall** (basis: mixed): Keep clear and keep children and pets inside. Call 000 if someone has a severe allergic reaction. Do not seal the hole or spray the hive.

## Plastering

- **Cracks in walls or ceilings** (basis: mixed): If the ceiling is sagging or the crack is rapidly widening, stay out from under that area and tell us now.
- **Plaster ceiling collapse or bad sag** (basis: mixed): Keep everyone out of the room. If water is coming through, turn off power to that area at the switchboard. Do not poke or push the ceiling.
- **Hole or damage in plasterboard** (basis: knowledge): If wires or pipes are visible inside the hole, do not touch them. Isolate power or water if anything is leaking.
- **Water-damaged, soft or bubbling plaster** (basis: knowledge): If water is still escaping, shut off the water at the meter or isolate the source. Keep clear of sagging ceilings and wet electrics.
- **Cornice or plaster joints cracked and gaps** (basis: mixed): If a cornice piece is loose overhead, keep out from under it.
- **Cracked or blown render** (basis: knowledge): Keep people and pets clear of loose render overhead.
- **Textured or popcorn ceiling removal** (basis: knowledge): If the home was built before about 1990, do not drill, sand or break this material. It may contain asbestos.

## Plumbing

- **Heat pump or solar hot water fault** (basis: sources): If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.

- **Gas continuous flow hot water error code or ignition failure** (basis: sources): Gas smell, hissing, soot or headache near a gas appliance: turn it off, open windows, get everyone outside, avoid switches and flames, call your gas emergency number, or 000 if anyone is unwell.
- **Hot water temperature fluctuates or cuts out (instant unit)** (basis: sources): If water bursts scalding hot, stop using that tap. If you smell gas, avoid switches and flames, open windows, leave, and call your gas emergency line or 000.
- **Hot water runs out too quickly or takes ages to arrive** (basis: sources): If water is leaking near electrics, switch the heater off at the switchboard only if safe and dry, and keep away from hot spray.
- **Hot water at one tap only or cold water from hot tap** (basis: sources): If any tap delivers scalding water, stop using it and keep children and elderly people away.
- **Gas storage hot water pilot light out** (basis: sources): If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.

- **Blocked toilet** (basis: mixed): Stop flushing and do not use affected fixtures. Keep children and pets away from sewage and wash hands well. If wastewater is overflowing inside, call a plumber urgently.
- **Leaking toilet** (basis: mixed): If water is near power points, the switchboard or appliances, keep clear and do not touch them. If safe, turn the water off at the meter or main tap. If anyone is at risk, call 000.
- **Burst or leaking pipe** (basis: mixed): If water is near power points, the switchboard or appliances, keep clear and do not touch them. If safe, turn the water off at the meter or main tap. If anyone is at risk, call 000.
- **Suspected hidden water leak** (basis: mixed): If you find water flowing and cannot stop it, turn the water off at the meter tap if safe. Keep clear of any water near electrics.
- **No water** (basis: mixed): If water is gushing anywhere, turn off the main tap if safe and keep clear of electrics. If there is no water and you rely on it for medical needs, call your water authority.
- **Washing machine or dishwasher connection** (basis: mixed): If water is near power points, the switchboard or appliances, keep clear and do not touch them. If safe, turn the water off at the meter or main tap. If anyone is at risk, call 000.
- **Flexi hose leak** (basis: mixed): If water is near power points, the switchboard or appliances, keep clear and do not touch them. If safe, turn the water off at the meter or main tap. If anyone is at risk, call 000.
- **Outdoor tap or garden irrigation fault** (basis: mixed): If a tap has snapped and water is flowing, turn off the water at the meter tap if safe and keep clear of electrics.
- **Shower or bath leak** (basis: mixed): If water drips near light fittings or the ceiling sags, keep clear and switch off power at the board if it is safe. Do not use the shower.

- **Water meter or main tap issue** (basis: mixed): If you cannot turn off the water in an emergency, do not force it. Keep clear of electrics and call a plumber or your water authority.

- **Water leaking through ceiling from above** (basis: mixed): Keep clear of a sagging ceiling. If water drips near lights, do not touch fittings; switch off power at the board only if safe. Turn the water off at the main if safe.

- **Pool pump not working** (basis: mixed): Do not touch the pump or electrics if the area is wet, smells burnt or trips the power. Switch off at the switchboard if it is safe, and keep children away.

- **Pool fence or gate not compliant** (basis: mixed): Keep the gate closed, supervise children at all times, and move chairs or toys away from the fence. If a child may be in the water, call 000.
