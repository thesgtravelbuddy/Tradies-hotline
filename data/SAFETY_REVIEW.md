# Safety message review


## Air Conditioning

- **Split system not cooling**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch the aircon off at the wall or isolator only if safe, keep clear of the unit and call 000 if there is fire.
- **Air conditioner leaking water**: If water is near power points or lights, or the ceiling is bulging, switch the aircon off at the isolator and the circuit breaker only if safe, and keep away from the wet area.
- **Air conditioner noisy**: If there is a burning smell, smoke or sparks, switch off at the wall or isolator only if safe, keep clear and call 000 if there is fire.
- **Air conditioner smells**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall or isolator only if safe, leave the area and call 000 if there is fire. If you smell gas, go outside and call the gas emergency number.
- **Air conditioner won't turn on**: If the breaker keeps tripping, or you smell burning, leave it switched off and do not keep resetting it. Call 000 if there is fire or smoke.
- **Air conditioner error code or flashing light**: If there is a burning smell or smoke with the fault, switch off at the wall or isolator only if safe and keep clear. Call 000 if there is fire.
- **Ducted air conditioning fault**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch the system off at the wall controller or isolator only if safe and call 000 if there is fire. Keep away from wet ceilings near lights.
- **Evaporative cooler problem**: If you smell burning, or water is near electrics, switch the cooler off at the wall or isolator only if safe. Do not climb on the roof.
- **Ice or frost on air conditioner**: Switch the aircon off at the remote to let the ice melt. If water is near electrics, switch off at the isolator only if safe.
- **Aircon tripping the circuit breaker**: Do not keep resetting the breaker. Leave the aircon off, and if there is a burning smell, sparks or smoke, keep clear and call 000.
- **Outdoor unit not running but indoor unit is on**: Do not touch the fan. If there is a burning smell or smoke, switch off at the isolator only if safe and call 000 if there is fire.

## Appliance Repair

- **Washing machine won't drain**: If water is near power points or the floor is wet around the machine, turn off the power at the wall only if the area is dry and safe. Turn the tap off. If unsure, stay clear.
- **Washing machine leaking**: If water is near power points or the floor is wet around the machine, turn off the power at the wall only if the area is dry and safe. Turn the tap off. If unsure, stay clear.
- **Washing machine won't start or spin**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall only if safe, then step away. If there is fire, call 000.
- **Noisy washing machine**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall only if safe, then step away. If there is fire, call 000.
- **Dryer not heating or not drying**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall only if safe, then step away. If there is fire, call 000.
- **Dryer won't turn on or drum won't spin**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall only if safe, then step away. If there is fire, call 000.
- **Dishwasher not draining**: If water is near power points or the floor is wet around the machine, turn off the power at the wall only if the area is dry and safe. Turn the tap off. If unsure, stay clear.
- **Dishwasher leaking**: If water is near power points or the floor is wet around the machine, turn off the power at the wall only if the area is dry and safe. Turn the tap off. If unsure, stay clear.
- **Dishwasher not cleaning or won't start**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall only if safe, then step away. If there is fire, call 000.
- **Fridge not cooling**: If you smell burning or see sparks, unplug only if it's dry and safe. Don't unplug the fridge if the area is wet. Move perishable food to a cooler.
- **Freezer not freezing or frosting up**: If melting water is near power points, switch off at the wall only if the area is dry and safe. Move frozen food to an esky.
- **Fridge leaking water or noisy**: If water is near power points or the floor is wet around the machine, turn off the power at the wall only if the area is dry and safe. Turn the tap off. If unsure, stay clear.
- **Oven not heating or temperature wrong**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall only if safe, then step away. If there is fire, call 000.
- **Cooktop or hotplate not working**: If the glass top is cracked, switch off at the isolator and don't use it. If you smell burning, switch off only if safe. For gas smell, see gas emergency entry.
- **Gas oven or cooktop smells of gas**: Do not use switches or flames. Open windows, turn off gas at the meter or bottle if safe, leave the house. Call your gas emergency line from outside. If fire or strong gas, call 000.
- **Rangehood not working**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall only if safe, then step away. If there is fire, call 000.
- **Microwave not working**: Stop using it. Unplug if safe. If smoke or fire, keep the door closed and call 000.
- **Appliance showing an error code**: If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the wall only if safe, then step away. If there is fire, call 000.
- **Fisher & Paykel DishDrawer error or beeping**: If water is near power points or the floor is wet around the machine, turn off the power at the wall only if the area is dry and safe. Turn the tap off. If unsure, stay clear.
- **Fridge ice maker or water dispenser not working**: If water is near power points or the floor is wet around the machine, turn off the power at the wall only if the area is dry and safe. Turn the tap off. If unsure, stay clear.
- **Induction cooktop error code or beeping**: If you smell burning or glass is cracked, switch off at the isolator and don't use it. Call 000 for fire.
- **Appliance tripping the power safety switch**: Do not keep resetting the switch. Unplug the appliance if safe. Call a licensed electrician if it persists. For smoke or fire, call 000.

## Carpentry

- **Door will not latch or close**: If an external door cannot be locked, tell someone nearby and keep valuables out of sight until it's fixed.
- **Sliding door stuck or off track**: Don't force a stuck door; it can come off its track. If it's an external door, secure it another way and tell someone.
- **Window stuck or will not open**: If a bedroom window that may be needed for escape is stuck shut, tell the tradie it is a safety priority.
- **Broken or cracked window glass**: Keep people and pets away from broken glass. Do not touch edges. If it was a break-in, contact police first.
- **Rotten timber (frames, fascia, windowsills)**: If timber supporting a roof, balcony or stairs is soft or sagging, keep people away from it.
- **Termite damaged timber**: Do not disturb active termite workings. Keep people away from sagging floors or damaged structure.
- **Decking repair or rot**: If a deck feels unstable or a rail is loose, keep people off the deck, especially children and the elderly.
- **Stairs or handrail loose or damaged**: Avoid using the stairs or leaning on the rail if it moves. Keep children and the elderly away until checked.
- **Wardrobe or sliding robe doors**: Do not lean on a loose mirrored door. Keep people and pets away if it could fall.
- **Squeaky or lifting floorboards**: If the floor feels like it will give way, keep people off that area.
- **Laminate or timber floor repair**: If water is still leaking, turn off water at the mains if safe to do so and call a plumber first.
- **Roof or pergola timber sagging**: Keep people away from under the structure, especially in wind or rain.
- **Door frame or jamb damage after break-in**: If you suspect a break-in, contact police first. Keep clear of damaged frames.
- **Water-damaged timber after a leak or flood**: If water is still coming in, turn off the water at the mains if safe, and call a plumber.

## Chimney & Fireplace

- **Wood heater or fireplace smoking into the room**: Put the fire out only if safe, open windows and doors, leave the room and call 000 if anyone is unwell or alarms sound. Do not use the heater until checked.
- **Chimney needs cleaning or sweeping**: Do not use the fireplace if you suspect a blockage or heavy buildup. Have the flue checked first.
- **Cracked or damaged chimney, flue or cap**: Keep people away from below a damaged chimney. Do not use the fireplace. If fumes enter the home, ventilate, leave and call 000.
- **Water leaking from chimney or fireplace**: If ceiling is bulging, keep out from underneath and place a bucket. Switch off power to lights in the area if wet.
- **Birds, possums or nests in the chimney**: Do not light a fire. Do not poke the flue. Keep pets away. Native wildlife may be protected, so contact a licensed handler or tradie.
- **Wood heater not working well or hard to light**: If smoke enters the room or an alarm sounds, ventilate, leave and call 000. Do not leave the heater burning unattended.

## Concrete

- **Uneven or sunken pavers**: Mark or barricade raised edges to avoid trips.
- **Subsidence or structural cracks**: If cracks are widening quickly or a wall looks to be leaning, leave the area and tell us now.
- **Concrete surface flaking or rust staining**: Keep clear of overhead concrete that may fall.
- **Concrete pooling water or poor fall**: If water is entering the home, move valuables up and keep clear of power points.

## Curtains, Blinds & Awnings

- **Venetian, Roman or cellular blind cord or tilt fault**: Cords can strangle children. Keep loops out of reach and remove cords from cots and beds until fixed.
- **Outdoor awning torn, stuck or not retracting**: Keep clear of a damaged or partly collapsed awning. Do not stand underneath. Keep pets and children away until secured.
- **Motorised blind or shutter not working**: If you smell burning or see sparks, switch off power to the blind at the wall and do not use it. Call an electrician.

## Damp & Mould

- **Sagging or bulging wet ceiling**: Keep everyone out of the room and away from the ceiling. Turn off lights below at the wall switch only if you can do it from a dry spot. Don't poke it. Call 000 if it falls.
- **Damp or mouldy walls**: Don't scrub dry or disturb large mouldy areas; spores spread. People with asthma, allergies or weak immunity, and young or older people, should stay out. Please don't climb to check roofs or gutters.
- **Rising damp**: Please don't scrape or sand damp walls or salt deposits in old homes; dust may contain old paint or other hazards. Keep stored items off wet floors and avoid damp skirting near power points.
- **Condensation and window mould**: Don't dry-brush mould, as that spreads spores. Anyone with asthma or weak immunity should stay clear. Don't use unflued gas heaters in sealed rooms.
- **Musty smell but no visible mould**: Don't cut into walls or ceilings to look. People with breathing problems should stay in another room. Please don't climb into the roof space to search.
- **Rain coming through external wall**: Keep power points and appliances away from wet walls, and don't use them if water is running down. Please don't climb to inspect the roof or gutters.

## Drainage

- **Blocked kitchen sink**: If water is leaking near power points or appliances, turn off power to that area. Do not mix drain cleaners, and tell the plumber which product you used.
- **Blocked bathroom, shower or laundry drain**: Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Blocked sewer drain or tree roots**: Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Sewage overflow**: Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends. If overflow is large or ongoing, also call your water authority's 24-hour line.
- **Septic tank or onsite system problems**: Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends. Keep stock and children away from wet patches.
- **Overflow relief gully bubbling or overflowing**: Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Blocked stormwater drain or pit**: Keep away from pooled water near power outlets or switchboards, and turn off power at the board only if it is safe to reach.
- **Overflow relief gully missing, buried or damaged**: Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Stormwater getting into the sewer, overflow in wet weather**: Sewage carries germs. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching it, wash hands well, and avoid using toilets, showers and taps until a plumber attends.
- **Sewer smell inside or outside the house**: If the smell is very strong or makes anyone dizzy or sick, ventilate and leave the area, then call emergency services if symptoms continue.
- **Sump or drainage pump not working**: If water is near power points or the switchboard, stay out of it and do not touch the pump; turn power off only if the switchboard is dry and safe to reach.

## Electrical

- **Safety switch keeps tripping**: If there is burning smell, sparks or water near electrics, keep clear and call 000 if fire. Do not touch wiring or wet fittings. Leave tripped circuits off until an electrician has checked them.
- **Power out to part of the house**: If you smell burning or see smoke or sparks, keep clear and call 000 if fire. Do not open wall plates or touch wiring. Report the issue to a licensed electrician.
- **Whole house power outage**: Stay well clear of fallen powerlines and anything touching them; call 000. If there is a burning smell or sparks, keep away. Only a licensed electrician should check wiring. Report street outages to your distributor.
- **Power point not working**: Do not use a power point that is hot, scorched, cracked or wet. Switch it off at the board if safe to reach. Do not open it or touch wiring.
- **Sparking or burnt power point**: Keep clear and do not touch. If safe to reach, switch off at the switchboard. If there is fire or smoke, leave and call 000. Never touch wiring or use the power point.
- **Burning smell from electrics**: If you see smoke or flames, leave and call 000. Otherwise, switch off at the switchboard only if safe to reach, keep clear, and call an electrician. Never touch wiring or hot fittings.
- **Lights flickering or dimming**: If you notice burning smell, sparking or crackling, switch off at the switchboard only if safe to reach and keep clear. Do not touch wiring. Call 000 if fire.
- **Light not working**: If the fitting is hot, scorched, wet or smells of burning, leave it switched off and keep clear. Do not touch wiring. Call 000 if there is fire.
- **Downlights tripping or failing**: If you smell burning or see smoke from the ceiling, switch off at the switchboard only if safe to reach, leave the area and call 000 if fire. Do not touch wiring or fittings.
- **Ceiling fan install or fault**: If the fan is loose, smells burnt or sparks, switch it off at the wall and the switchboard only if safe, keep people clear and call 000 if fire. Do not touch wiring or wobbling parts.
- **Switchboard upgrade or old fuse box**: Do not open the switchboard or touch components. If there is burning smell, sparks or heat, stay clear, switch off the main switch only if safe to reach, and call 000 if fire.
- **No safety switch fitted**: Safety switches protect against shock but may not cover every circuit. Do not open the board. If you get any shock or tingle, stop using that item and call a licensed electrician.
- **Smoke alarm beeping or not working**: If you see or smell smoke or fire, leave and call 000. Do not remove or disable alarms. If one is sounding unexpectedly, keep everyone safe and arrange an electrician.
- **Power cut: house or street problem**: Stay well clear of fallen powerlines and anything touching them; call 000. Report outages to your distributor using the number on your bill. Only licensed electricians should check wiring.
- **Storm or flood damage to electrics**: Stay clear of fallen powerlines and floodwater near electrics; call 000. Do not switch on wet circuits or appliances. A licensed electrician must check wiring after storm or flood damage.
- **Buzzing, humming or crackling electrics**: If buzzing comes with burning smell, heat, sparks or smoke, leave it switched off if safe, keep clear and call 000 if fire. Do not open fittings or touch wiring.
- **Shock or tingle from tap, appliance or fitting**: Do not touch or re-test the item; keep everyone away. Call 000 if anyone is hurt. Report the shock or tingle to your electricity distributor and arrange a licensed electrician. A tingle is a warning.
- **Hot switch, plug or cord**: Unplug or switch off only if safe and dry, stay clear, and keep cords unwound. Do not stack plugs. If smoke or burning, call 000. Do not touch wiring.
- **Damaged wires, meter box or service line**: Stay well clear of fallen or low powerlines and anything they touch; call 000 and your distributor. Do not touch, prune trees or approach. Only authorised workers should fix supply lines.
- **Generator connection or backup power**: Never connect a generator to your home wiring yourself. Only a licensed electrician may do it. Run generators outdoors, away from windows, and never plug them into a power point.
- **Install extra power points**: If you see burn marks, smell burning or the socket feels hot, switch off at the board and keep clear until an electrician checks it.
- **Oven or cooktop hardwiring**: If the old oven or cooktop smells of burning, sparks or trips the power, switch it off at the board and do not use it until checked.
- **Outdoor lighting or power**: Keep away from wet or damaged outdoor fittings and cables. Switch off at the switchboard if safe. Do not touch exposed wires.
- **Solar system not producing**: Only read the inverter screen or app. Do not open the inverter, touch roof wiring or climb up. If burning smell, scorching or arcing, keep clear and call 000.
- **Generator install or fault**: Never run a generator indoors, in a garage or enclosed space. Never plug it into a wall socket or the switchboard. If anyone feels dizzy or unwell, get into fresh air and call 000.
- **Storm or lightning damage**: Stay 8 to 10 metres from fallen powerlines and call 000 and your network. Do not touch damaged cables, wet switchboards or solar equipment. Do not turn power on if damage is visible.
- **Electric shock or tingle**: If anyone is hurt or unwell, call 000. Do not touch the appliance, tap or switchboard. Unplug only if dry and safe, switch off at the board, and call a licensed electrician.
- **Hot water element or electric hot water fault**: If water is leaking near electrics or you smell burning, switch off the hot water circuit at the board and keep clear. Do not open covers.
- **Powerboard overload / blown fuse**: If you smell burning or see scorch marks, switch off at the board, unplug items if safe and keep clear. Do not keep resetting a breaker that trips again.
- **Solar panels damaged by hail or storm**: Do not climb on the roof or touch damaged panels or cables. If it is safe, follow the shutdown label at the inverter. Call a licensed electrician and photograph from the ground.
- **Dim or flickering lights across the house**: Do not touch the switchboard or metal in the home. Turn off easy-to-reach power points, unplug appliances and call your electricity distributor immediately.
- **Safety switch won't reset**: If the safety switch will not reset, do not keep trying. Call a licensed electrician. Do not touch the board if wet or burnt-smelling.
- **Damaged solar battery**: Do not switch on a damaged battery and treat it as live. Keep clear and call a licensed electrician. If smoke or fire, leave and call 000.

## Fencing

- **Fence leaning or falling over**: Keep people and pets away from any unstable fence. If it has fallen onto a pool barrier or road, secure the area or ask a neighbour.
- **Storm-damaged fence**: Stay clear of fallen trees and fences near power lines. Keep children and pets away from broken areas.
- **Gate sagging or not closing**: If the gate is part of a pool fence and does not latch, keep children away from the pool until it's repaired.
- **Retaining wall problem**: Keep people, children and pets away from below and above the wall. Do not dig or add weight near it.
- **Pool or balcony safety barrier repair**: Keep children away from the pool or balcony until the barrier is fixed. Supervise closely or block access temporarily.

## Flooring

- **Timber floor cupping, lifting or gaps**: If water caused it, avoid slipping on wet floors and keep electrical items away from damp areas. Switch off power if water reached outlets.
- **Damaged or water-damaged floor**: Do not walk on soft or sagging floors. If water is flowing, turn off the mains tap. Keep power off in wet areas and call 000 for flood danger.
- **Vinyl or lino floor lifting, cracked or tiles loose**: Do not sand, scrape or break up old vinyl or lino tiles in a house built before about 1990. They may contain asbestos. Keep the area closed off.
- **Floor tiles cracked, loose or lifting**: Mark the lifted tile edge so no one trips. If water is leaking, turn off the mains water and tell the tradie.
- **Carpet or flooring wet or smelly after leak**: Keep out of wet areas near power. Switch off affected circuits at the board if safe. Raw sewage means keep everyone away and call a plumber.

## Garage Doors

- **Garage or roller door remote not working**: Keep people and pets clear of a moving door. Do not stand under a half-open door.
- **Garage or roller door won't open or close**: Stay clear of a stuck, sagging or partly fallen door. Do not touch springs or cables. Keep people and pets away.
- **Garage door spring or cable broken**: Do not touch the spring, cables or door. Keep people and pets clear and do not stand under it. Call a technician.
- **Garage door off track or crooked**: Do not push or lift a jammed or crooked door. Keep clear and keep people and pets away.
- **Garage door reverses when closing**: Don't stand under or reach into a door that is stuck or unbalanced. Keep children and pets away. Never override safety sensors yourself.
- **Garage opener motor hums but door won't move**: Do not use the opener if it grinds or the door looks unbalanced. Keep clear of the door.

## Gas

- **Gas smell or suspected leak**: If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **Gas appliance not working**: Gas smell, hissing, soot or headache near a gas appliance: turn it off, open windows, get everyone outside, avoid switches and flames, call your gas emergency number, or 000 if anyone is unwell.
- **Gas heater fault or fumes concern**: Headache, dizziness, nausea or confusion near a gas appliance may be carbon monoxide. Turn it off, open doors and windows, get everyone outside, and call 000 or Nurse-on-Call 1300 60 60 24.
- **LPG bottle or regulator problem**: If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **New gas point or gas appliance install**: If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **Portable or BBQ gas bottle leaking or no gas flow**: If you smell gas or hear hissing, put out flames, keep sparks away, turn the bottle valve off only if safe, move people away and call the supplier emergency number or 000.
- **Gas heater hose or fitting will not connect**: If you smell gas, do not use switches or flames, open windows, turn off at the bottle or meter if safe, leave the area and call your gas supplier or emergency services.

## General Triage

- **Something is wrong but I don't know the trade**: If you smell gas, see sparks or smoke, or water is pouring in, make yourself safe first. Leave the house for gas, and call 000 for fire or serious danger.
- **Strange smell**: If you smell gas, leave the house, don't touch switches, and call your gas emergency number from outside. If you smell burning or see smoke, switch off at the board if safe and call 000.
- **Water stain on ceiling**: If the ceiling is bulging or dripping near lights, stay out of the room and switch off at the board only if safe. A bulging ceiling can collapse: don't stand beneath it.
- **Noise in the walls**: If there is crackling, buzzing at a power point, or a burning smell, switch off at the main board if safe and keep clear. If a hissing pipe is wetting walls, turn off the water main.
- **Storm damage**: Stay at least 8 metres from fallen wires and anything they touch. Storm help: SES 132 500. If life is in danger or there is fire, call 000. Do not touch a damaged switchboard.
- **Power or water cut in part of the house**: Do not touch a damaged or wet switchboard. If you smell burning, see sparks or water near electrics, keep away and call 000. After storms, call a licensed electrician.
- **Damp or mould on walls**: Avoid scrubbing large mould areas or breathing dust, especially with asthma or breathing problems. If water is near power points, keep clear.
- **Cracks in walls or floors**: If a crack is wide, growing quickly, or a wall is leaning or bulging, keep out of that area and call a builder or structural engineer.
- **Smell of gas**: Leave the house now. Don't use switches, phones, or flames inside. From outside, call your gas emergency number or 000. Turn off at the meter or bottle only if outside and easy.
- **Hidden leak or high water bill**: If water is flowing near electrics or flooding, turn off the main tap, and the power if safe. Keep away from wet switchboards and cords.
- **Water coming in after rain**: Stay clear of water near power points and don't walk through floodwater. For flood help call SES 132 500; if life is threatened, call 000.
- **Something stopped working**: If there's a burning smell, sparks, or water near power, don't touch it; switch off at the board if it's safe and dry. For gas smells, leave and call your gas emergency number.
- **It's making a funny noise**: If the noise comes with burning smell, smoke, or sparks, switch it off at the wall or board only if safe, and keep clear. For gas appliances with a gas smell, leave and call your gas emergency number.
- **Damp patch on wall, floor or carpet**: If water is near power points or lights, keep clear and switch off at the board only if dry and safe. If a ceiling is sagging with water, stay out from under it.
- **Door or window won't close properly**: If an external door or window cannot be locked or closed, move valuables and ask a neighbour or friend to be aware until fixed.

## Glazing & Windows

- **Broken or cracked window glass**: Keep people and pets clear of the broken glass. Do not pick up shards with bare hands. Cover with cardboard or a thick sheet if safe. Wear shoes.
- **Window will not open or close**: If a window is the only emergency exit from a room and is stuck, tell the household and avoid using that room to sleep until fixed.
- **Shower screen glass cracked or shattered**: Keep everyone out of the bathroom until cleaned safely. Wear shoes. Do not walk through glass fragments. Cover broken edges. Call 000 for serious injury.
- **Mirror cracked, loose or needs replacing**: Keep clear of any mirror that is loose or partly detached. Do not lean on it. If safe, tape an X across the face to hold pieces if it breaks.
- **Sliding door glass broken or door off track**: Keep people and pets clear of the broken glass. Do not pick up shards with bare hands. Cover with cardboard or a thick sheet if safe. Wear shoes.

## Gutters

- **Gutter replacement**: Please don't climb a ladder to hold up or fix hanging gutters. Keep people clear beneath any gutter that is sagging or pulling away. Old cement-type gutters shouldn't be broken or cut by anyone.
- **Fascia or eaves rotting or damaged**: Please don't climb up to push or pull at the boards. Keep people clear under sagging or falling timber. Don't sand, drill or scrape old eaves sheeting; it may contain asbestos.
- **Leaf guard or gutter guard installation**: Please don't climb a ladder to clean or fit guards yourself. Never lean ladders on gutters. Old cement-type gutters or roofing shouldn't be touched or broken.

## Handy Services

- **Gutter cleaning**: Do not climb ladders or step onto roofs; wet gutters are slippery.
- **Rubbish and hard waste removal**: Do not break or handle suspected asbestos sheets.

## Handyman

- **General handyman odd jobs list**: If any job involves gas smell, exposed wiring or flooding, treat it as urgent and call the right emergency trade.

## Heating

- **Gas heater not working**: Do not use it. Turn it off if safe, open windows and doors and leave the room. If anyone feels sick or smells gas, go outside and call 000 or your gas emergency number.
- **Gas heater safety check**: If anyone feels dizzy, has a headache or nausea while the heater is on, turn it off, open windows and doors, go outside for fresh air and call 000 if unwell.
- **Electric heater or wall panel fault**: If you smell burning or see scorching, turn it off at the switch and unplug only if safe, keep clear and call 000 if there is smoke or fire.
- **Ducted heating fault**: If you smell gas or anyone feels dizzy or sick, turn the heater off if safe, open doors and windows, go outside and call 000 or the gas emergency number.
- **Gas heater pilot or ignition fault**: Do not use it. Turn it off if safe, open windows and doors and leave the room. If anyone feels sick or smells gas, go outside and call 000 or your gas emergency number.
- **Wood heater problem**: If smoke fills the room, leave, ventilate if safe and call 000 if there is fire. If anyone feels dizzy, sick or has a headache, go outside into fresh air and call 000.
- **Suspected gas leak or fumes from heater**: Turn the heater off if safe, open windows and doors and get everyone outside. Do not use switches or flames. Call 000 or your gas emergency number. Do not re-use the heater until checked.

## Insulation & Draughts

- **Insulation old, damaged, wet or disturbed**: Do not disturb loose-fill insulation or dusty material in older homes. Keep out of the roof space and keep children away.
- **Downlights and insulation clearance**: If you smell burning or see scorch marks, turn off the light circuit at the switchboard and call an electrician. Call 000 if smoke or fire.

## Landscaping & Irrigation & Paving

- **Retaining wall or garden bed build**: Keep people clear of any retaining wall that is leaning or cracking.
- **Sprinkler zone not working or leaking**: If water is near power points or entering the house, switch off the water supply at the mains.
- **Pavers sinking or uneven**: Mark or cover major trip hazards until repaired.
- **Garden drainage and boggy yard**: If water is entering the house, protect electrical items and call an emergency plumber.

## Locksmith

- **Locked out of home**: If a child, baby, unwell or elderly person is locked inside, call 000 and ask for the fire brigade. Do not force the door or break glass.
- **Broken key in lock**: Do not keep forcing the key or use glue or tools in the lock. If a child, pet or unwell person is locked inside, call 000.
- **Key won't turn or lock is stiff**: Do not force the key as it may snap. If someone is trapped inside, call 000.
- **Door won't latch or handle is loose**: If a child or unwell person is locked in a room, call 000. Don't force the door in a way that could injure anyone.
- **Keypad or smart lock not working**: If a child or vulnerable person is locked inside, call 000.
- **Locks after break-in or tenant leaving**: If an intruder may still be present, leave and call 000.

## Painting

- **Lead paint concern in older home**: Homes built before 1970 may have lead paint. Do not dry sand or scrape it. Keep children and pregnant women away from dust.

## Pest Control

- **Cockroaches**: Keep food covered and pets away from any area where pest products have been used.
- **Rats or mice**: Keep pets and children away from baits and traps. If you smell burning or suspect chewed wiring, switch off at the board only if safe and call an electrician.
- **Ants**: Keep food covered and pets away from treated areas. Don't try to burn or flood nests.
- **Termites or white ants**: Don't disturb or spray a live termite nest or mud tubes; this can send them elsewhere. Leave the area undisturbed for the inspector.
- **Spiders**: Suspected funnel-web bite (large dark spider): call 000, keep the person still, apply a firm bandage. Child, elderly or severe pain, sweating or breathing trouble from any bite: call 000. Do not catch the spider.
- **Wasps or bees nest**: If stung and unwell, with swelling of face or throat or trouble breathing, call 000. Keep away from nests and swarms, and keep children and pets clear. Do not spray or knock the nest.
- **Possums in the roof**: Possums are protected native wildlife; do not trap, poison or harm them. Sick or injured one: call a wildlife rescue group. Burning smell or sparks: switch off at the switchboard and call an electrician.
- **Fleas, ticks or mites**: Tick on a person: do not pull or squeeze it; seek medical advice. Trouble breathing, face swelling or collapse after a bite: call 000.
- **Mosquitoes or flies outdoors**: If anyone has a fever, rash or joint pain after mosquito bites, see a doctor.
- **Birds or bats roosting**: Bats and many birds are protected wildlife; do not harm them. Do not handle a bat with bare hands; call a vet or wildlife carer and see a doctor if scratched or bitten.
- **Snakes or lizards near the house**: Snake bite, even if painless or no marks: call 000, keep the person completely still, firmly bandage the limb. Keep clear of the snake; do not try to catch or kill it. Keep pets and children away.
- **Fire ants suspected**: Do not touch or poke the nest; fire ants swarm. If stung with trouble breathing, facial swelling or faintness, call 000. Report suspected fire ants on 13 22 68 (NSW: NSW Government).
- **Flying termite swarm indoors**: Do not spray or disturb the area. Switch off lights, keep the swarm sealed in one room if possible, and keep a sample or photo.
- **Dead animal smell in wall or roof**: If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas emergency line. Do not enter sealed roof spaces without protective gear.
- **Bee swarm or hive in a wall**: Keep clear and keep children and pets inside. Call 000 if someone has a severe allergic reaction. Do not seal the hole or spray the hive.

## Plastering

- **Cracks in walls or ceilings**: If the ceiling is sagging or the crack is rapidly widening, stay out from under that area and tell us now.
- **Plaster ceiling collapse or bad sag**: Keep everyone out of the room. If water is coming through, turn off power to that area at the switchboard. Do not poke or push the ceiling.
- **Hole or damage in plasterboard**: If wires or pipes are visible inside the hole, do not touch them. Isolate power or water if anything is leaking.
- **Water-damaged, soft or bubbling plaster**: If water is still escaping, shut off the water at the meter or isolate the source. Keep clear of sagging ceilings and wet electrics.
- **Cornice or plaster joints cracked and gaps**: Keep away from ceilings that sag or crack suddenly. Do not use rooms that feel unsafe.
- **Textured or popcorn ceiling removal**: If the home was built before about 1990, do not drill, sand or break this material. It may contain asbestos.
- **Torn or damaged ceiling near exhaust fan**: If the ceiling is sagging, bulging or dripping, keep people out from under it. Keep clear of wet light fittings and power points.

## Plumbing

- **No hot water - electric storage**: If water is leaking near electrics, or there is a burning smell or sparks, switch the hot water off at the switchboard only if dry and safe, keep clear, and call 000 if there is fire or shock risk.
- **No hot water - gas storage or instantaneous**: Gas smell, hissing, soot or headache near a gas appliance: turn it off, open windows, get everyone outside, avoid switches and flames, call your gas emergency number, or 000 if anyone is unwell.
- **Hot water system leaking**: If water is near electrical wiring or the unit is electric, switch it off at the switchboard if safe, turn off the water supply to it, and keep people away until a licensed tradie attends.
- **Noisy hot water system**: If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **Heat pump or solar hot water fault**: If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **Hot water too hot or tempering valve issue**: If water is scalding hot, do not use that tap and keep children and elderly people away. Mix cold water before use and call a plumber urgently.
- **Gas continuous flow hot water error code or ignition failure**: Gas smell, hissing, soot or headache near a gas appliance: turn it off, open windows, get everyone outside, avoid switches and flames, call your gas emergency number, or 000 if anyone is unwell.
- **Hot water temperature fluctuates or cuts out (instant unit)**: If water bursts scalding hot, stop using that tap. If you smell gas, avoid switches and flames, open windows, leave, and call your gas emergency line or 000.
- **Hot water runs out too quickly or takes ages to arrive**: If water is leaking near electrics, switch the heater off at the switchboard only if safe and dry, and keep away from hot spray.
- **Hot water at one tap only or cold water from hot tap**: If any tap delivers scalding water, stop using it and keep children and elderly people away.
- **Gas storage hot water pilot light out**: If you smell gas or hear hissing: do not use switches, phones near it, or flames. Open doors and windows, leave, turn off gas at the meter only if safe, then call your gas emergency number or 000.
- **Leaking mixer tap or tap base**: If water is spraying or pouring and near electrics, keep clear and turn the water off at the valve under the sink or the main tap if safe.
- **Blocked toilet**: Stop flushing and do not use affected fixtures. Keep children and pets away from sewage and wash hands well. If wastewater is overflowing inside, call a plumber urgently.
- **Leaking toilet**: If water is near power points, the switchboard or appliances, keep clear and do not touch them. If safe, turn the water off at the meter or main tap. If anyone is at risk, call 000.
- **Burst or leaking pipe**: If water is near power points, the switchboard or appliances, keep clear and do not touch them. If safe, turn the water off at the meter or main tap. If anyone is at risk, call 000.
- **Suspected hidden water leak**: If you find water flowing and cannot stop it, turn the water off at the meter tap if safe. Keep clear of any water near electrics.
- **No water**: If water is gushing anywhere, turn off the main tap if safe and keep clear of electrics. If there is no water and you rely on it for medical needs, call your water authority.
- **Washing machine or dishwasher connection**: If water is near power points, the switchboard or appliances, keep clear and do not touch them. If safe, turn the water off at the meter or main tap. If anyone is at risk, call 000.
- **Flexi hose leak**: If water is near power points, the switchboard or appliances, keep clear and do not touch them. If safe, turn the water off at the meter or main tap. If anyone is at risk, call 000.
- **Outdoor tap or garden irrigation fault**: If a tap has snapped and water is flowing, turn off the water at the meter tap if safe and keep clear of electrics.
- **Shower or bath leak**: If water drips near light fittings or the ceiling sags, keep clear and switch off power at the board if it is safe. Do not use the shower.
- **Frozen or cold-damaged pipes**: Do not use a naked flame on pipes. If a pipe has split, turn off the water at the main tap if safe and keep clear of electrics.
- **Water meter or main tap issue**: If you cannot turn off the water in an emergency, do not force it. Keep clear of electrics and call a plumber or your water authority.
- **Sewer smell inside**: If sewage is backing up, stop using fixtures and keep children and pets away. Open windows for ventilation and call a plumber promptly.
- **Dishwasher or laundry drain overflow**: Stop using the machine and taps. If water is near power points or appliances, keep clear and switch off at the board only if the area is dry and safe.
- **Main sewer blockage or multiple drains backing up**: Stop using toilets, showers and taps. Keep children and pets away from sewage and wash hands well. Call a plumber urgently.
- **Water leaking through ceiling from above**: Keep clear of a sagging ceiling. If water drips near lights, do not touch fittings; switch off power at the board only if safe. Turn the water off at the main if safe.
- **Hot water relief valve dripping or will not close**: Do not touch the discharge pipe, the water can be scalding. Keep clear, and switch the unit off at the switchboard if safe. A licensed plumber must attend.

## Pool

- **Green or cloudy pool water**: If you can't see the pool floor, keep children and pets away. Don't mix pool chemicals, and keep them out of reach.
- **Pool pump not working**: Do not touch the pump or electrics if the area is wet, smells burnt or trips the power. Switch off at the switchboard if it is safe, and keep children away.
- **Pool filter problem**: If a filter tank looks cracked, bulging or leaking hard, switch the pump off at the switchboard and keep people away until it is checked.
- **Pool leak or losing water**: Keep away from wet ground near electrical gear and do not drain the pool yourself. Draining can damage the shell; wait for a pool professional.
- **Salt chlorinator fault**: If the chlorinator unit smells burnt, is hot or sits in water, switch it off at the switchboard if dry and safe, and keep children away.
- **Pool heater not heating**: If you smell gas near the heater, do not use switches or flames; move away and call your gas emergency number or 000. Burning smell or smoke: switch off power if safe and call 000.
- **Pool fence or gate not compliant**: Keep the gate closed, supervise children at all times, and move chairs or toys away from the fence. If a child may be in the water, call 000.

## Roofing

- **Roof leaking into the house**: Please don't climb onto the roof or lean a ladder on it to look. Keep away from any wet or sagging ceiling and stay clear of water near lights or power points.
- **Broken or cracked roof tiles**: Please don't go on the roof to fix or check tiles; people fall and tiles break underfoot. Keep people and pets away from the area below any loose or hanging tiles.
- **Rusty or lifting roof sheets**: Please don't climb on the roof. If sheets are loose, stay clear underneath. Older cement-type sheets may contain asbestos: don't break, drill, scrub or pressure-wash them.
- **Ridge capping loose or cracked**: Please don't go on the roof. Loose ridge tiles can fall; keep people and pets away from beneath the roof edge until a roofer has looked.
- **Storm or hail damage to roof**: Stay off the roof and out of damaged rooms. Do not touch fallen power lines or anything near them; call 000. For urgent storm help call the SES on 132 500. Keep clear under damaged ceilings.
- **Skylight leaking**: Please don't climb onto the roof to reseal it. Keep clear of any wet or sagging ceiling, and stay away from water near lights or power points.
- **Chimney or flashing leak**: Please don't climb up to the chimney. If bricks look loose or leaning, keep people away from beneath it. Don't light the fireplace if water is coming down it.
- **Roof insulation problem**: Please don't go into the roof space yourself. It can be hot, hard to move in, and old insulation or sheeting may contain asbestos. Don't step between ceiling joists. Keep clear of any wet ceiling.
- **Box gutter or valley leaking**: Please don't climb onto the roof or lean a ladder on it to look. Keep away from any wet or sagging ceiling and stay clear of water near lights or power points.
- **Roof vent or whirlybird problem**: Please don't climb onto the roof. If a vent is hanging off, keep people and pets away from beneath it. Keep clear of wet ceilings.
- **Roof restoration or repaint quote**: Please don't pressure-wash or climb the roof yourself; that's a common cause of falls and damage. Old cement sheeting may contain asbestos and must not be scrubbed or sprayed.
- **Roof sheeting that may be asbestos cement**: Do not touch, break, drill, sweep, scrub or pressure-wash old sheeting, and keep children and pets away from broken pieces. Don't climb onto it. Wait for a licensed assessor.

## Security Systems

- **Alarm beeping or showing a fault**: If you think someone has broken in, leave and call 000. If the panel smells of burning, switch it off at the power board if safe.
- **Alarm false alarms or going off by itself**: If you suspect an intruder, stay out and call 000. Otherwise silence at the keypad if you know the code.
- **Gate or door access control fault**: If anyone is trapped or a fire exit is blocked, call 000.
- **Sensor lights or security lighting**: If a fitting is sparking or water-logged, turn it off at the switchboard and stay clear.

- **Solar inverter replacement**: If the inverter smells burnt or sparks, switch off solar isolators if safe, keep clear and call an electrician.

- **TV antenna install or replacement**: Stay clear of antennas near powerlines and call the power company. Do not climb on the roof.

## Tree & Garden

- **Dangerous tree**: Keep people, pets and cars away from beneath and around a tree that looks ready to fall. If it is near or touching power lines, stay 8 metres back and call the electricity emergency number or 000.
- **Fallen tree or branch after storm**: Stay at least 8 metres from fallen wires and anything they touch, and call the electricity emergency number or 000. If the tree is on the house, keep people out. Storm help: SES 132 500; life-threatening: 000.
- **Tree touching or near power lines**: Stay at least 8 metres (about 2 car lengths) from fallen wires and anything they touch, and keep others away. Call your electricity network's emergency number, or 000 if anyone is hurt or there is fire.
- **Tree or hedge pruning**: Do not climb or cut anything near power lines yourself. Keep clear and call the electricity network or a qualified arborist.
- **Lawn and garden maintenance**: If you have seen a snake or sharp rubbish in overgrown areas, keep children and pets away and let the contractor know before work starts.
- **Irrigation or sprinkler problems**: If water is gushing, turn off the main tap or irrigation isolation valve if you can do so safely. Keep away from wet electrical controllers and cords.
- **Tree roots damaging pipes or paths**: If lifted paving is a trip hazard, mark it off and keep children and elderly people away until it is made safe.
- **Neighbour's tree overhanging or dropping branches**: If a large dead limb is hanging over your home or play area, keep people away from underneath it and let us know.

- **Bathroom floor leaking**: Stop using the bathroom if water is leaking out. If water has reached power outlets, turn off power to the area.
- **Balcony or deck waterproofing leak**: If the ceiling is sagging, bulging or dripping, keep people out from under it. Keep clear of wet light fittings and power points.
- **Bathroom leak causing damp in neighbouring room**: If water is near power points, lights or the switchboard, keep clear and turn the power off at the main switch only if it is dry and safe to reach. Call 000 if there is danger.
